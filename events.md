# Events

View a list of upcoming public and reserved events at Jackson's Mill.

We welcome visitors to WVU Jackson's Mill. Please note however, there are times when our campus is closed to the general public, like when youth activities or other camps are being held on our grounds. Your understanding is appreciated.

Check out a listing of upcoming events, and if you'd like more information about what's happening at Jackson's Mill, you can call 304-269-5100 or email Jacksons.Mill@mail.wvu.edu. Keep in touch by subscribing to our email newsletter and follow us on social media.
